## Ethanol Density Calculator

Calculates the density of pure ethanol or ethanol-CO₂ mixtures. Pure ethanol density uses the **Poling et al.** correlation. When a CO₂ fraction is specified, the **Linear Blend Rule** combines pure component densities:

ρ_mix = x_CO₂ · ρ_CO₂ + (1 − x_CO₂) · ρ_EtOH

The CO₂ density component is calculated via the Bender EoS at the specified pressure and temperature.
