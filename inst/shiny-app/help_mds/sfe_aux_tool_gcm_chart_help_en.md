## GCM Method Selection Chart

This chart shows which Group Contribution Methods (GCMs) are available for estimating each thermophysical property. Use it as a reference when selecting methods in the Solute Characterization module.

### Properties

**Normal boiling point** (*T*<sub>b</sub>) — estimated by Joback-Reid, Stein-Brown (and their corrected variants), Nannoolal (2004), and Hukkerikar methods.

**Critical temperature** (*T*<sub>c</sub>) — estimated by Joback-Reid, Nannoolal (2007, including a robust variant), and Hukkerikar methods.

**Critical pressure** (*P*<sub>c</sub>) — same methods as *T*<sub>c</sub>.

**Critical volume** (*V*<sub>c</sub>) — same methods as *T*<sub>c</sub>.

**Hansen Solubility Parameters** (*δ*<sub>d</sub>, *δ*<sub>p</sub>, *δ*<sub>HB</sub>) — estimated by Stefanis-Panayiotou (2008, 2012) and Hukkerikar methods.

**Van der Waals volume** (*V*<sub>vdW</sub>) — estimated by Zhao (2003), Bondi (1964), and Slonimskii (1970) methods.

A checkmark (✔) in the table indicates that the method supports estimation of that property.
