## General Mixture Critical Parameters

Calculates the critical temperature and pressure for arbitrary multi-component solvent mixtures using thermodynamic mixing rules.

**Critical temperature methods:** Li, Chueh-Prausnitz
**Critical pressure methods:** Chueh-Prausnitz, Kreglewski-Kay

Select solvents from the built-in database, specify their composition in mass or mole fractions, and choose the mixing rules to apply. The mixture must contain at least two components.
