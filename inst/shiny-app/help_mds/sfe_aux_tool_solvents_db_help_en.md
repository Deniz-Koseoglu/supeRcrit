## Available Solvents Database

This table lists the thermophysical properties of solvents and co-solvents available for use in the Miscibility Optimization, Miscibility Comparison, and Critical Parameters modules.

### Displayed Properties

**Identifying information:** Solvent name, abbreviation, molecular formula, and molecular weight (*M*, g/mol).

**Critical parameters:** Critical temperature (*T*<sub>c</sub>, K), critical pressure (*P*<sub>c</sub>, bar), critical volume (*V*<sub>c</sub>, cm³/mol), and acentric factor (*ω*).

**Hansen Solubility Parameters (HSP):** Dispersion (*δ*<sub>d</sub>), polarity (*δ*<sub>p</sub>), and hydrogen bonding (*δ*<sub>HB</sub>) components (all in MPa<sup>0.5</sup>), as well as the total solubility parameter (*δ*<sub>t</sub>).

**Molar volume** (*V*<sub>m</sub>, mL/mol) at standard conditions.

All solvent data are compiled from the CoolProp library (**Bell et al., 2014**) and literature sources.
