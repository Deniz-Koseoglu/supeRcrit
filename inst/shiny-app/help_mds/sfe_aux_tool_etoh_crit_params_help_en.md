## CO₂-Ethanol Critical Parameters

Calculates the critical temperature and critical pressure of binary CO₂-ethanol mixtures as a function of composition.

**Available methods:**
- **Chueh-Prausnitz:** Uses binary interaction parameters and pure component critical properties.
- **Redlich-Kister:** Polynomial expansion approach for mixture critical properties.

The **Validation Plot** button generates a diagram comparing predictions across the full composition range against published experimental data, allowing you to assess the accuracy of each method.
